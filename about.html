Our story

About KwK

One creative kid, one big imagination.

Krafting with Kay began with a simple idea: helping one creative kid turn her imagination into something real.

What started as family craft projects quickly grew into passion for creating, learning, and sharing handmade treasures with others. Inspired by love of nature and hands-on creativity, Kaylee began making crafts that brought joy to family, friends, and her community.

Every workshop starts the same way it always has: with everyday materials, a little imagination, and the belief that what you make with your hands matters.

At Krafting with Kay we believe creativity can change the world, one project at a time.
