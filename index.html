Meet Kay

Crafting with heart

Kay runs Krafting with Kay out of Aurora, Colorado — a craft studio built on upcycling, creativity, and community. Old jars become glowing lanterns, scraps become fairy gardens, and every Wednesday the doors open for anyone who wants to make something with their hands.

Her work has a soft spot for teachers, gifters, and crafters at heart — and gift cards are always available for the holidays.
